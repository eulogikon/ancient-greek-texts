# Suda

*Σοῦδα*

**eul_aid:** `wus` · [HTML page](https://eulogikon.org/authors/suda-wus)

The Suda is a 10th-century Byzantine lexicon of ~31,000 entries covering Greek vocabulary, biography, history, and geography. Edited by Ada Adler.
