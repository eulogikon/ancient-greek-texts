Anonymous Philosophical Treatise (PHerc. 1667)
by Anonymous Author of PHerc. 1667

Dialect  Koine (phi, frg)
Domain   Philosophy
Format   Treatise

PHerc. 1667 is the surviving inner core of an anonymous philosophical roll. Angelotti et al. report the complete virtual unwrapping of its preserved surface and publish a transcription of the lower portions of columns 5-22; columns 1-4 preserve only traces. The title and author are lost. The text discusses natural impulses, completion, practical wisdom, crafts, appropriate actions, goods and happiness. Its vocabulary and the appearance of Aristocreon, Chrysippus' relative and pupil, support a probable Stoic context but do not establish an attribution. The papyrus is dated to the second century BCE, with a very late third-century date not excluded.

This corpus entry follows the column-level Greek transcription in G. Angelotti et al., Complete virtual unwrapping and reading of a rolled Herculaneum papyrus, arXiv:2606.29085v1 (2026), pp. 22-27. Brackets and underdots preserve the editors' uncertainty notation; lunate sigma alone is normalized to the corpus encoding.
