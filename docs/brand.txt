The reading site is https://eulogikon.org
The catalog and agent entry is https://eulogikon.github.io/ancient-greek-texts
The data store is https://github.com/eulogikon/ancient-greek-texts
In citation the DOI form is preferred: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20335421

[boilerplate]
Eulogikon is a free, open-source digital library of ancient Greek texts. It publishes 4,060 works by 1,358 authors — Homer through late antiquity — under Public Domain Mark 1.0. No login, subscription, or payment is required.
