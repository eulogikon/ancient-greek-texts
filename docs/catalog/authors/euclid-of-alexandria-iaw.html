Euclid of Alexandria

Εὐκλείδης ὁ Ἀλεξανδρεύς

Hellenistic · Mathematics · 17 works

Euclid of Alexandria, Hellenistic mathematician of the early third century BCE, authored the *Elements*, which systematized plane geometry and number theory. The work underpinned mathematical education for more than two millennia across Mediterranean and European cultures. Eulogikon offers his 17 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
