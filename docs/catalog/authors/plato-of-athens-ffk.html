Plato of Athens

Πλάτων ὁ Ἀθηναῖος

Classical · Philosophy · Platonist · 40 works

Plato of Athens, student of Socrates and founder of the Academy, stands at the center of Western philosophy. Dialogues such as the *Republic*, *Symposium*, and *Phaedo* explore justice, love, and the immortality of the soul with lasting intellectual force. Eulogikon offers his 40 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
