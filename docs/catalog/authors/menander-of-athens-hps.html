Menander of Athens

Μένανδρος ὁ Ἀθηναῖος

Hellenistic · Comedy · 43 works

Menander of Athens, leading playwright of New Comedy in the Hellenistic age, portrayed everyday life, love, and social intrigue in plays such as *Dyskolos*. His character-driven comedy shaped Roman drama and later European theater traditions. Eulogikon offers his 43 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
