Hesiod

Ἡσίοδος

Archaic · Epic · 7 works

Hesiod, archaic Greek poet of about 700 BCE, composed the *Theogony* and *Works and Days*, cornerstones of Greek mythology and didactic poetry. He systematized the divine pantheon and offered practical counsel on farming, justice, and human toil. Eulogikon offers his 7 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
