Porphyry of Tyre

Πορφύριος ὁ Τύριος

Late Antique · Philosophy · Neoplatonist · 27 works

Porphyry of Tyre, Neoplatonist philosopher and pupil of Plotinus, edited the *Enneads* and wrote the *Isagoge*, a standard introduction to Aristotelian logic. His scholarship bridged Plato, Aristotle, and religious philosophy across late antique philosophical curricula. Eulogikon offers his 27 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
