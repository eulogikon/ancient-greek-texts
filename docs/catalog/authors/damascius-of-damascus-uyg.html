Damascius of Damascus

Δαμάσκιος ὁ Δαμασκηνός

Late Antique · Philosophy · Neoplatonist · 7 works

Damascius of Damascus, last scholarch of the Athenian Academy in late antiquity, wrote *Problems and Solutions Concerning First Principles*, a searching inquiry into Neoplatonic metaphysics. His work marks the close of pagan philosophical teaching in Athens. Eulogikon offers his 7 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
