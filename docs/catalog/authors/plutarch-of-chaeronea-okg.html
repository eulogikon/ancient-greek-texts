Plutarch of Chaeronea

Πλούταρχος ὁ Χαιρωνεύς

Roman · Philosophy · Platonist · 147 works

Plutarch of Chaeronea, Middle Platonist biographer and moralist of the Roman period, wrote the *Parallel Lives* and the *Moralia*. His portraits of Greek and Roman leaders shaped historical imagination and ethical thought for centuries afterward. Eulogikon offers his 147 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
