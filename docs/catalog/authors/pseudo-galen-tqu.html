Pseudo-Galen

Ψευδο-Γαληνός

Late Antique · Medicine · 25 works

Pseudo-Galen gathers medical writings transmitted under Galen's name but of uncertain or composite authorship in later antiquity. These texts document how Galenic medicine—alongside tracts such as *On the Pulse*—was adapted and taught after the classical corpus took shape. Eulogikon offers 25 such works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
