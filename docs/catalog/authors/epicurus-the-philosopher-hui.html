Epicurus the Philosopher

Ἐπίκουρος ὁ φιλόσοφος

Hellenistic · Philosophy · Epicurean · 8 works

Epicurus, Hellenistic philosopher and founder of the Garden school in Athens, taught an ethics of prudent pleasure and freedom from disturbance. Letters such as the *Letter to Menoeceus* address the gods, friendship, and the fear of death. Eulogikon offers his 8 works as PDF, Markdown, and TXT.
